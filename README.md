# FinTech & AI Portfolio
### Large Data & Statistical Learning | Ongoing Academic Portfolio

This repository documents my ongoing coursework, practical assignments, and projects in **Large Data and Statistical Learning**, with a focus on applications in Financial Technology (FinTech), Artificial Intelligence, and data-driven decision-making.

The portfolio serves as a record of my learning journey, combining theoretical foundations with hands-on implementation, data analysis, and statistical modelling.

As the course progresses, the repository will be continuously updated with new assignments, experiments, presentations, and projects.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this portfolio are to:

- Develop practical skills in processing, managing, and analysing large datasets.
- Explore statistical learning techniques and their real-world applications.
- Apply Python-based analytical methods to structured data.
- Strengthen the connection between statistical theory, computational methods, and financial applications.
- Document methodological decisions, experimental results, and key learning outcomes.
